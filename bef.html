Business Entity Formation

To start a business entity you need to make the right choice that is why we are here to help.

Business entities consist of corporations and limited liability companies. These business entities provide some legal protection of stockholders and members/partners personal assets. Making the right choice of entity is important. The proper fit for an enterprise depends on a number of factors that we will discuss with you in detail and informing you about the tax implications for each entity.

The successful formation and operation of a business entity has directly to do with the notion that individuals should be given the opportunity to put forth an idea, to develop a plan, to create a product, or to provide a service, and to be able to do so in a way that frees up needed capital. That helps insure that the operation will be properly financed.

Forming the right business entity will help reducing risks; protect assets, not just for today, but also into the future.

We will assist in the proper selection of an entity, the formation, the tax impact and provide the tools necessary for its success.
