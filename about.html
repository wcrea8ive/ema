Our Mission

EMA Accounting provides accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, audit, consulting, tax, and advisory services to many businesses. Our associates work diligently with one purpose in mind: to deliver effective, measurable and lasting results.

We help reinforce our clients' financial stability and inspire them to make their most challenging business decisions with confidence, and help lead the way toward a stronger economy and a healthy society.

Our clients count on EMA Accounting to help them transform uncertainty into possibility and rapid change into lasting progress. Our associates know how to anticipate, collaborate, innovate, and create opportunity from even the unforeseen obstacle.

What We Offer

Efficiency

With over 12 years with big four accounting firms, Ernst & Young, Deloitte and Coopers & Lybrand, and over 15 years of hands-on experience with small to mid-size companies, we offer tailored solutions that will help meet the challenges and opportunities of building a better business in this fast-paced working world.

Motivation

We'll start by getting to know you. You do the talking, we'll do the listening then we will develop a plan to guide you and your business to achieve the desired goals. This is what we do and why we exist as a firm.

Aptitude

Our team of well qualified and skilled associates will work with your business management and individuals to develop in-depth knowledge and understanding of your financial needs and provide the necessary tools to achieve the values you are looking for.
